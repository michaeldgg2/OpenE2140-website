- Vehicles/aircraft can move/land on trees and pipes non non-clear terrain
- More fixes for Heavy Lifter crashes

- Change tooltip of civilian buildings to *Civilian building* ([#781](https://github.com/OpenE2140/OpenE2140/pull/781)) by Dzierzan
