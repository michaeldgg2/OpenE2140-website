# devtest-20260606
([Full changelog](#full-changelog) below)

## Lobby
- Rare crash when changing map: incorrect slot assignments for players or bots can occur and lead to a crash with an empty exception log.

## Skirmish AI
- Save/load not implemented: bot module state is reset on load, causing behavior differences between new and loaded games.

### Unit production
- Bots do not set rally points for newly produced units at production buildings.

### Base building
- Bots cannot construct Water Bases
	 - A special 2v2 custom map (*Naval Warfare 2 Teams*) contains pre-built Water Bases,
	 - However bots are unable to rebuild them if they're are destroyed.
- Sub-optimal placement that partially blocks elevators or Mine/Refinery conveyor belts.
- Occasional conflicting deploy orders for MCUs cause them to block each other and repeatedly search for new deployment locations.
- Base defenses placed without proper prioritization, especially around Mines and Refineries.
- Other building placement issues

### Economy management
- Rigid transport assignments: bots enforce a strict 2:1:1 ratio (2 Banthas/Heavy Lifters per Mine and Refinery), producing several issues:
	- After a Mine is depleted, bot doesn't reassign Bantha/Heavy Lifter to other Mines.
	- **Active bug**: assignment state can degrade, producing imbalanced or inefficient configurations (e.g., three Banthas servicing one Mine/Refinery while another has one), or causing long-distance transport routes.
- Poor tactical trade-offs: bots struggle to choose between expanding the economy and building combat units or defenses
	- For example they may continue expansion attempts when defending or attacking would be preferable
- No threat-aware expansion: bots do not evaluate enemy proximity or danger when placing Mines.
- Refineries placed with poor pathing consideration: although a Refinery may be close in straight-line distance to its Mine, ground route for Banthas is long (e.g., Refineries built on hills behind the base on 'Beyond Destruction').

### Special abilities/units
- Miners do not place walls
- Screamers and Shadows are not utilized properly:
	- Shadows don't position themselves properly to support attacking/defending squads
	- Screamers do not use their reveal ability tactically (e.g., to probe for cloaked enemies while avoiding exposure).
- Bots don't build any transporter units (TUR, ATM 500, BIO, HAT, WTRN, WTP 100)
